Scenario: a blocked rail connection

C1 must reach rail by minute 12. C2 sits above it and must move first. C3 is due by minute 18 but awaits outbound authorization. Both cranes share a work area and one transfer lane. Fail YC1, inspect recovery suggestions, reassign queued work, then dispatch moves and advance time.

Completion here means simulated delivery to the rail buffer—not a confirmed real train departure. All durations and constraints are teaching assumptions.
